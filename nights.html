Divi Garba 2026 · 11 — 20 October

Ten nights,
nine forms.

Navratri counts nine nights, and each one belongs to a form of Durga — nine names, in an order that has not changed in a very long time. Divi keeps all nine and adds a tenth for Dussehra. This is what each night is, and what it is like on the ground.

Nights 1 — 9 are 11 to 19 October. The tenth, Vijayadashami, is 20 October.

Night 1Sun 11 Oct

શૈલપુત્રી

Shailaputri

Daughter of the mountain

The first form, and the plainest: the mountain's daughter, standing. Everything the next nine nights build needs ground to stand on, and this is the night that lays it. The plot is swept, the lamp is lit, and the circle is drawn for the first time.

Night 2Mon 12 Oct

બ્રહ્મચારિણી

Brahmacharini

The one who walks in devotion

The long penance — barefoot, a water pot, years of it. The second night is the patience the other eight are built on. Nobody dances well on a first night; the step is learned here, in the repetition nobody watches.

Night 3Tue 13 Oct

ચંદ્રઘંટા

Chandraghanta

The bell of the moon

A half moon on her brow shaped like a bell, and a tiger beneath her. This is the night the circle finds its nerve — the dhol stops asking and starts telling, and the ground answers it.

Night 4Wed 14 Oct

કૂષ્માંડા

Kushmanda

The little cosmic egg

She smiled, and the sun was lit. The fourth night carries that warmth: the ground holds its heat long past midnight, and the circle stops being something you joined and becomes something you are inside.

Night 5Thu 15 Oct

સ્કંદમાતા

Skandamata

Mother of Skanda

The mother with her child on her lap, seated on a lion. The fifth night is shelter. Half of what a garba ground is for is being somewhere safe enough to lose yourself, and somebody is always watching the edges so that you can.

Night 6Fri 16 Oct

કાત્યાયની

Katyayani

Born of the sage Katyayana

Riding the lion, and armed. The sixth night is the fast one: the step turns hard, the circle tightens, and everyone on the ground knows it without being told.

Night 7Sat 17 Oct

કાલરાત્રિ

Kalaratri

The night that is time itself

The darkest form, and the kindest. She is frightening to look at and takes the fear off whoever looks. The seventh night runs latest and quietest, and the ground is never emptier or fuller than it is then.

Night 8Sun 18 Oct

મહાગૌરી

Mahagauri

Radiantly white

Washed clean of the ash of the seventh night. The eighth is the composed one — lamps set in a ring, the ground at its stillest, everything about to be given.

Night 9Mon 19 Oct

સિદ્ધિદાત્રી

Siddhidatri

Granter of every power

The lotus opens. Every siddhi granted, the ninth circle closed, and the last turn of a Navratri that began nine nights ago on a swept and empty plot.

Night 10Tue 20 Oct

વિજયાદશમી

Vijayadashami

The tenth, and the loudest

Navratri counts nine, and the tenth belongs to Dussehra — the victory the nine nights were building toward. There is no tenth form of Durga to show, so this gateway is left open and lit. It is the night Divi runs hardest, and the one the ground remembers.
